# Intentional differences from the original

What the port changes on purpose, and what each change does to original behaviour. Evidence for the original side is in [`original-behavior.md`](original-behavior.md). Addresses: `S:` or none is `spel.dat`, `M:` is `menu.exe`, `L:` is `A2Racer4.exe`.

A row says what the port does. It does not say the row was verified: what was checked, and on what, is in [`validation.md`](validation.md) and [`feature-parity.md`](feature-parity.md). All checks of the port ran on etappe 0, Amsterdam, in headless Chromium or in Node, apart from the boot check of all etappes and one launch of track 9 with car 7. The reference runs of the original ran on Amsterdam, under Wine and, for the 8-bit path, on an emulated Windows 98 machine. No real phone, tablet or gamepad, no other browser engine, and sound was never judged by ear. Differences from the two reference ports are in [`reuse.md`](reuse.md).

## Time

| Change | Original | Port | Effect on behaviour |
|---|---|---|---|
| Simulation rate | One pass of the main loop per rendered frame, at whatever rate the machine gives, with a smoothed `dt`. Measured under Wine: nothing in the program limits the rate except `Flip`. Under Xvfb with llvmpipe, where `Flip` does not wait, it runs at 317 to 393 ticks per second, and on a clean clock the gas pedal hardly rises there (highest speed 1.25 to 12.7 m/s in 12 s) | Fixed 60 passes per second. `dt` comes from `hook!updateClock`, see Clock ring | Reasoning in `original-behavior.md`, timing section. It was not possible to establish what rate the developers tuned at. Held to 60 ticks per second from outside, the original and the port agree by movement state: [`validation.md`](validation.md). What the owner's fixes part 13 covers runs as it did at 25 FPS: chase camera, skid marks, smoke, police brake, car lighting and the rest in `tools/recomp/owner-fixes/README.md`. Other logic that runs per pass stays at its 60-tick values. Against a machine at 30 FPS: the copalert level falls from 100 to 0 in 0.25 s against 0.5 s; particle counts differ (bases 15 and 35 give 9 and 29 against 4 and 24); AI re-placement tries at 20% per tick, so 12 times a second against 6; cylinder pops draw one or two `rand()` per pass and radio states step once per pass, so the random stream and radio timing differ too |
| Clock ring | `FUN_004a6700` fills a 4-entry ring from a float counter. The first sample holds the counter's whole value at clock init, the second the time `L` from clock init to the first tick. Ticks 1 to 3 are capped at 0.1 s on any machine up for more than a second, tick 4 only if `L` is above about 0.68 s at 60 Hz. Measured under Wine at 60 ticks: `L` 8.42 ms, `dt` in ticks 1 to 6 0.1, 0.1, 0.1, 0.0158, 0.0185, 0.0166, tick 4 not capped at any rate tried. Clock noise puts `dt*300` just under or over 5, so the truncating gas step of `FUN_0046f2a0` mixes +4 and +5: 21 to 23 ticks to full gas | `hook!updateClock` in `source/host/race.js` keeps the ring, so race start and dialog returns read as in the original. First sample: the clock's value at init. Second: `INIT_TO_FIRST_TICK`, 8.4 ms, as measured. Every later sample is exactly 1/60 s. A steady ring gives `dt` one tick with its last bit alternating, in place of the clock noise | `dt` in ticks 1 to 6: 0.1, 0.1, 0.1, 0.0156, 0.0167, 0.0167. Physics delta in ticks 4, 5, 6: 0.0789, 0.0665, 0.0582 against 0.0790, 0.0669, 0.0585, so the physics lead at GO is close to the original's. GO at pass 283 against 280 or 281, since this `dt` has no noise. The alternation is deliberate and measured, not noise: `dt*300` falls just under and over 5, so the gas step mixes +4 and +5, 23 ticks to full gas (4, 9, 13, 18, 22, 27...) against 21 to 23 in the original (5, 9, 13, 18, 22, 27...). With a fixed `dt` it took 19 ticks (5, 10, 15...). 8.4 ms is that machine's `L`: another machine will differ, and a machine with `L` above about 0.68 s would cap tick 4 |
| Traffic pushes | `FUN_00428950` and `FUN_00415E40` push traffic and path vehicles once per frame with no time step, so traffic gets stronger with the frame rate up to 66 FPS and lifts its nose | Scaled by `min(1, tick * 25)`, as the owner's `A2-Racer-4-Fixes` part 9 does (`traffic_mass`): one whole push per 1/25 s | Highest nose rise at 60 ticks with whole pushes 0.742, against 0.74 in the owner's table for the original under Wine. With the scale 0.208, against 0.18 for his patched game under Wine. The means cover more starts of more kinds than his, so they are not comparable. So this follows the patched game, not the unpatched one. His `trailer_push` is not applied: that code has no route in the shipped game |
| Rendering rate | Same as simulation | Browser's rate, no cap. World matrices, the camera and moving vertices blend between the last two ticks. A matrix that turns more than 20 degrees in a tick, such as a wheel at speed, keeps the latest tick's rotation and blends only its position, so a wheel keeps its size and shape. The camera cuts only when its eye jumps more than 30 units in one tick, as on a camera switch. `gfx_wolk` smoke, `gfx_bwlk` and all particles including sparks show the latest tick, because their slots are re-sorted or shift, so nothing can be paired across ticks | Up to one tick (16.7 ms) of added visual latency. On displays above 60 Hz smoke, `gfx_bwlk` and particles step at 60 Hz while the rest moves smoothly. Blob shadows blend with their car. The player's shadow moves with its car: its polygons of the latest tick are carried from the car's pose of that tick to the car's blended pose, so it stays where the game put it under the car. Also not blended: skid-mark strips whose vertex count changed, and any moving draw whose first vertex moved more than 30 units. Under a pause or quit dialog and at the end of a race the picture stays on its key tick's frame |
| Falling behind | `dt` capped at 0.1 s, so game time slows while `GetTickCount` timers keep real time, and a slow machine loses race time | Elapsed time capped at 0.25 s per frame and at most 6 ticks per displayed frame, the rest dropped. `dt` stays one tick | Same direction: game time slows rather than jumps. Race timers slow with the game instead of keeping real time. No check reached this: the benchmark held 60 FPS |
| FPS gates | `FUN_0041cae0` skips AI re-placement below 15 FPS. `FUN_0043dd90` and `FUN_00446c70` skip spawns at about 12. `FUN_0043de00` and `FUN_00446fe0` wipe effects below 10. `FUN_0044cb30` lowers visible chunks by 1 per tick below 10, down to 15 or 25 | `dt` reads 0.1 for the first 3 ticks and about one tick from tick 4 on, whatever the device does | The gates do not fire after the start, even when the browser falls behind. `FUN_0044cb30` needs state 0 and the first ticks are in state 1, so the view distance governor never fires. View distance and effects stay full on a slow device: the price of a fixed tick. The 8-bit path, where this shows most: see 8-bit path: slow machines |
| Focus loss | Message pump blocks while inactive. Wall-clock race timers keep running, and the first ticks after the return are capped | Ticks and the virtual clock stop, sound suspends, keys and touches release; the clock does not jump on return | Silence while away, as in the original (inferred from DirectSound's documentation), but sound positions stand still here. Timers differ on purpose: a hidden tab cannot lose a race |
| Pause and quit dialogs | Only the race clocks stop (`FUN_0044ca80`). `GetTickCount` runs on | The same: `Race.tick` advances the virtual clock during a dialog | No difference. Traffic light, timed bonuses, HUD messages, respawns and the reset lockout run on, so a timed bonus can run out during a pause. Recorded because Focus loss and Settings panel do stop these timers |
| Settings panel | None | Opening the race panel stops ticks, the clock and sound, and releases all input. The focus moves inside, so keys stay out of the race. Esc and F9 close it, and the closing key is not the game's until released | The picture stands still. Time in the panel counts for nothing |
| Clocks | Windows counters from boot | One virtual clock driven by ticks. `hook!updateClock` feeds the frame clock's ring from it, see Clock ring. Code that spins on the clock still leaves: `query()` adds 0.5 ms per call after 2000 calls in one tick | Removes the precision loss described in the owner's patch notes, part 9. Measured under Wine at 31 h uptime: `dt` 0.0141 to 0.0201 at 60 ticks, against 0.0160 to 0.0173 on a clean clock |
| Random seed | `time()` and `GetTickCount` | Same calls, real time. `?bench=1` and the Node tools fix them for repeatable runs | None in play |

**The owner's patch, decision.** Since 1.1.0 the race code runs, besides no-CD and the traffic share (part 9, `traffic_mass`, see Traffic pushes), the parts of his `A2-Racer-4-Fixes` 1.5.4 that change what the game does at 60 ticks: parts 11, 12 and 13. His own added code is lifted with the rest of `spel.dat`, from his bytes: `tools/recomp/owner-fixes/`. So at 60 ticks the race follows his patched game on purpose, not unpatched 1.01:

| Change | Original 1.01 at 60 ticks | Port | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hung cars, part 11 | A car caught on a ramp, a bridge part or its roof stays there; only a spinning car is corrected | Still for 3 s with an axle off the ground or tipped over: back on the road by the game's own reset | Racers no longer stay stuck until the race ends. A car can still hang for those 3 s |
| Body contacts, part 12 | A contact's check that it is still over its triangle is used up once per frame by `FUN_004F3810`; at 60 ticks some contacts outlive their triangle and push a car up an invisible plane | Every contact is checked on every physics step, and moving an object ends its contacts | No nose-up climb from that cause. Crossing between triangles the body sags about 0.09 units before it collides again, as at low frame rates in the original |
| The reset, part 12 | Leaves the waypoint list, blocks Enter after any car's reset, stops with `Illegal chunk` at the last section when lanes look taken, and can place a car under the road on etappes 5 and 6 or on a tunnel on 7 | Cleans up, wraps around, uses the lane's height where the surface found is more than 25 units off | Enter waits only after Enter. No `Illegal chunk` from this. Resets land on the road |
| Per-frame effects, part 13 | Police brake, Supercop pedals and patrol, chase camera, car lighting, portrait outline, skid marks, smoke, mud and water, pickups, animated objects, debris, letters, speed camera flash and turbo tank work per pass | As at 25 FPS: a 25 Hz tick for what counts sections, fades and sprays, `min(1, dt * 25)` for what scales | The camera lags more than at 60 per pass, skid marks last about 2 s, smoke lasts longer, cars reach the road's light, animations step at the rate their time gives |

Left out, with reasons: his display, transparency, Z, colour depth, clock and uptime parts, which the port does its own way, and the parts that matter only above 66 or 300 frames per second. The list is in `tools/recomp/owner-fixes/README.md`.

| 8-bit path: loading page | The game draws the PCX of `[wait_lo]` or `[wait_hi]` and the bar from `overlay.pcx` through its palette, in `race.boot()` | The page shows the same PCX pictures through the game's palette, by a PNG made ahead (`eNN-lo.png`, `eNN-8.png`), and the bar from `overlay.pcx` (`bar8-NN.png`) | `race.boot()` shows nothing until it returns, as with a 3D card, see Loading page. Not compared with the guest |
| 8-bit path: slow machines | Below 10 FPS `VISIBLECHUNKS` falls to its floor of 15 (`FUN_0044cb30`), and below 12 FPS smoke stops spawning (`FUN_00446c70`). Saves already lowered to Normaal by an older value stay there until the player changes them |
| Hand-edited `FOG` | `fog=0` ships and the menu has no such option. With a hand edit of `game.ini`, `FOG` and `rand() & 1` at start set fog colour `0xB4B4C8`, linear 3.5 to 1000 (`FUN_00457040`); the game computes no per-vertex factor | Not supported | The shader mixes by specular alpha, which is 0 on nearly all vertices, so the picture would turn a flat colour. Not reachable from the menu |
| Hand-edited `mipmapping` | `[GFX OPTIONS] mipmapping=1` builds chains from authored levels. Only a hand edit reaches it: the menu's checkbox goes into the save only | Not supported: `Load` uses level 0 only. ## Numerics

x87 code runs on doubles, not the 80-bit format. See [`recompiler.md`](recompiler.md). What precision the original ran at is not established, because `FUN_004aa0c0` sets a reserved precision-control value, and 53 bits is this port's choice. So runs are not bit-identical to Windows. No check compares numbers with a native trace. Under Wine the first three ticks agree with this model to about 7 digits, with the control word not read: [`evidence/reference-wine/results.md`](../evidence/reference-wine/results.md).

## Port settings that the original did not have

**3D-resolutie**, **Renderschaal** (25 to 200%), the resulting picture size with a `GPU-limiet` note, **Volume**, **Beeldjes per seconde tonen**, **Volledig scherm**, **Aanraakbediening** (Automatisch, Aan, Uit) and **Deadzone gamepad**. In the race's settings panel (gear button or F9), and in the menu under Opties, **Geavanceerd**, tab **Browser**. They apply at once and are saved per browser in `localStorage`, key `a2racer4.settings`. Annuleren or the x of Opties or Geavanceerd puts them back. They are not part of a player's save. Turning Aanraakbediening on during a keyboard race gives a note and works from the next race, because the game binds its device once at load; turning it off works at once.

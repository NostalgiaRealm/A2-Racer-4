# A2 Racer 4 v1.1.7 — the other cars' shadows

After the tail-light fix in [1.1.6](1.1.6.md), every object the game draws was checked for the same fault. One more was affected: the round shadows under the other cars. They are one shape that the game moves under each car in turn, and the port matched them between ticks by the order they were drawn in. When a car came within range that order shifted, and a shadow was drawn a tick ahead of its car for a moment. Each shadow is now matched with the nearest one of the tick before, so it stays under its car. All ten etappes were checked: nothing else is matched wrongly.

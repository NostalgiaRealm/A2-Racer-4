# A2 Racer 4 v1.1.5 — the HUD in spray

Driving through water or mud with a lot of spray, the HUD could disappear for a moment. Every spray particle needs a slot in the renderer, and a dense spray used up all 1,024 of them before the HUD was drawn; the HUD then landed off screen until the spray thinned out. There are now 4,096 slots, more than the game's spray can fill.

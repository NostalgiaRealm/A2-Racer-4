# A2 Racer 4 v1.1.4 — Chromium on Wayland

In Chromium on a native Wayland desktop, such as KDE Plasma or GNOME, the road, the ground and the sky were drawn black. The game's picture was right, but the page's canvas had a transparency channel that the game's drawing leaves partly empty, and Wayland showed those parts as see-through. The canvas is now opaque. Other systems never showed it.

# A2 Racer 4 v1.1.2 — the player's shadow

The shadow under your car no longer jitters. The game cuts the shadow against the road again every tick, into new polygons, while the browser port draws the picture between the last two ticks to run smoothly at any display rate. The shadow could not be matched between ticks, so it stayed at the latest tick while the car moved on, and slid back and forth under it. It now moves with the car, as on Windows and under Wine, where car and shadow come from the same tick.

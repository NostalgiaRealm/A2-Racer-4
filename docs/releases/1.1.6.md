# A2 Racer 4 v1.1.6 — steady tail lights

The glow of the tail lights could flicker while driving, mostly on a fast display. The browser port draws the picture between the last two game ticks, and has to work out which object of one tick is which object of the next. All car lights share one shape, and the port matched them by the order they were drawn in, which changes whenever a car or a light comes into view. A tail light was then matched with another light and drawn a tick ahead of its car for a moment. Now each light, and each car that shares a model with others, is matched with the nearest one of the tick before.

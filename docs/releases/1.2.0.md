# A2 Racer 4 v1.2.0 — the whole screen on a phone

On a phone held upright the race now fills the whole screen. Until 1.1.7 it took the top 62 percent, and the touch controls stood on a black panel below. The wheel and the buttons now lie over the view, and the Speedometer and the Communicator stand just above them.

The touch buttons are fewer. Camera, Pauze, Dashboard, Detail and Meer are gone from the screen. Roepen is a round button beside Claxon, Turbo and Handrem. Reset and Stop are in the gear's panel as "Auto terugzetten" and "Race stoppen"; Stop still opens the game's own prompt with Ja and Nee. Terugkijken stays. With Pauze gone, the gear's panel is the pause on a touch screen: the clock stops while it is open. Keyboard and gamepad controls are unchanged.

The menu and the loading screen are 4:3 pictures. What they leave open on a wider or taller screen is no longer black: the same picture lies behind them, blurred and dimmed. Movies keep black beside them.

# Touch-control artwork

Copied from the RedCat SuperKarts port, `public/assets/touch/`, whose notice follows. This port uses the two SVGs only, and they are byte-identical to that project's: `tools/prepare-assets.py` records their SHA-256 in `public/assets/asset-map.json`. The unmodified source drawing and its manifest stay in that project (`public/assets/touch/sources/`), and its generated bonus button is not used here.

`steering-wheel.svg` and `brake-pedal.svg` are extracted from **Car Controls**
by **looneybits**, published on 5 September 2015 under **CC0 1.0 Universal**.

- Author's release: <https://opengameart.org/content/car-controls>
- Original SVG: <https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/car_controls.svg>
- License: <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>
- Local license text: [CC0-1.0.txt](CC0-1.0.txt)

Adaptations: extracted wheel group `g9211` and brake-pedal group `g37281`;
removed the blue sheet background, unrelated objects, editor metadata and
unused gradient definitions; fitted separate transparent SVG view boxes.
The original vector paths, colours and gradients of each control are retained.
No SuperTuxKart artwork is included in these SVGs.

CC0 does not require attribution; this notice records the source and makes the
editable artwork and its provenance easy to find. It applies only to these
control assets, not to the original game assets or the whole project.
